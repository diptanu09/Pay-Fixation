## 3. Pension & Family Pension Formulae

| Benefit Component | Formula / Percentage | Statutory Limit / Cap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Superannuation Pension** | `(Last Basic Pay × 50%) × (Qualifying Half-Years / 66)` | Capped between Min ₹9,000 and Max 50% Highest Pay |
| **Enhanced Family Pension** | `Last Basic Pay × 50%` | Payable for 10 years or up to Age 67 of deceased employee |
| **Normal Family Pension** | `Last Basic Pay × 30%` | Minimum ₹9,000 per month |
| **Commutation Lump Sum** | `(Basic Pension × Commutation %) × 12 × Age Factor` | Maximum 40% of Basic Pension |
